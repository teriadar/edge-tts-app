Initial release of Pagina Reader:
• EPUB 2 and EPUB 3 parsing via native Rust engine (rust-core)
• Neural Edge-TTS audio narration with English and Italian voices
• Real-time word-by-word highlighting and click-to-read navigation
• Background playback with AndroidX Media3 (ExoPlayer)
• Local SHA-256 audio caching and automatic book cover/metadata lookup
